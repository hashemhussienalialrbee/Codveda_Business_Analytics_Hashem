## Task 1 – Power BI Customer Churn & Retention Analytics

This task presents an interactive Power BI dashboard focused on customer churn and retention analysis. The dashboard summarizes key customer KPIs and highlights churn patterns across states, international-plan customers, and customer service call frequency.

### Dashboard Overview

The Power BI report contains two main analysis pages:

1. **Customer Churn & Retention Analytics**
   - Provides an executive overview of customer churn KPIs.
   - Includes interactive filters for State, International Plan, and Voice Mail Plan.
   - Visualizes churned customers by state.
   - Compares churn rate by international plan.
   - Analyzes churn rate by customer service calls.
   - Includes a business insight highlighting the relationship between service-call frequency and churn.

2. **Business Insights & Recommendations**
   - Summarizes the main analytical findings.
   - Provides business recommendations for customer retention.
   - Documents the key limitation of the analysis.

### Key KPIs

- **Total Customers:** 2,666
- **Churned Customers:** 388
- **Active Customers:** 2,278
- **Overall Churn Rate:** 14.55%
- **Average Account Length:** 101
- **Average Customer Service Calls:** 1.56

### Key Findings

- The overall customer churn rate is **14.55%**, with **388 churned customers out of 2,666 customers**.
- Customers with an **International Plan** show a substantially higher churn rate (**43.70%**) compared with customers without an International Plan (**11.27%**).
- Higher customer service call frequency is associated with substantially higher churn rates in the analyzed dataset.
- Churn levels vary across states, highlighting the importance of regional customer-retention analysis.

### Business Recommendations

1. **Investigate International Plan Churn**
   - Review pricing, service quality, usage patterns, and customer experience among International Plan customers.

2. **Improve Customer Support Resolution**
   - Investigate recurring customer issues and improve support resolution for customers with frequent service calls.

3. **Develop Targeted Retention Strategies**
   - Identify higher-risk customer segments and prioritize targeted retention efforts.

4. **Monitor Churn Regularly**
   - Use the interactive dashboard to monitor churn KPIs and identify changes across customer segments.

### Data Limitation

The findings represent associations observed in the analyzed dataset and should not be interpreted as proof of causation. Further analysis would be required to identify the underlying causes of customer churn.
